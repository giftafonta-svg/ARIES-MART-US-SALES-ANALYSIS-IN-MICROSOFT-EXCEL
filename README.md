# ARIES-MART-US-SALES-ANALYSIS-IN-MICROSOFT-EXCEL
Analysis of a ~10,000-row US retail sales dataset sourced from Kaggle, covering orders from 2014–2017. Raw data includes order date, customer name, state, category, sub-category, product name, sales, quantity, and profit. It includes:
Sales by Sub-Category — total sales broken down across 17 product sub-categories 
Monthly Sales — sales trend across all 12 months to spot seasonality
Top 5 Customers — highest-profit customers ranked
Sales by State — full state-level sales breakdown across all 49 US states/territories in the data
Unique Customer Count — count of distinct customers by year (2014–2017)
Dashboard — a single-view summary combining the above into visual charts
Tools/skills used: Excel PivotTables, SUMIFS/COUNTIFS-style aggregation, and dashboard/chart design.
